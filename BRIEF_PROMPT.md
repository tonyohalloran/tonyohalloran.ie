# Prompt for web Claude (claude.ai) — personal site design brief

Copy everything below the line into claude.ai. Paste the result back into the
Claude Code session as `BRIEF.md` (or just paste it in chat).

---

I'm rebuilding my personal website (tonyohalloran.ie) with Claude Code and I
want it to be genuinely *mine* — both my work identity and who I am as a
person, not a generic researcher template. You have access to my chats and
memory, so I want you to act as my biographer and produce a **design brief**
the coding agent will use to design the site with me.

Important ground rules:

- **Privacy filter**: this brief will shape a public website. Only include
  things I'd plausibly be comfortable publishing. Leave out anything about
  unpublished/in-progress research details, employers' internal work, other
  people's private information, health, finances, or relationships. If
  something feels borderline, put it in a separate "ASK TONY FIRST" list at
  the end rather than in the brief itself.
- **No invention**: if you don't actually know something about me, say
  "unknown" — don't fill gaps with plausible-sounding filler. Mark anything
  you're inferring (rather than something I've said) with "(inferred)".
- Be specific and concrete. "Likes cycling" is weak; "did X ride, talks about
  Y" is useful.

Produce the brief in this structure:

## 1. Identity & voice
How I come across in my chats: tone, humour, how formal/informal, pet
phrases, how I explain things. 5–10 adjectives plus 2–3 example sentences
that "sound like me" (paraphrased is fine).

## 2. Work (public-facing)
What I do and what I care about professionally, at the level I'd describe it
to a stranger at a conference: themes, questions that animate me, what I'd
want a visitor (potential collaborator, employer, journalist) to take away in
30 seconds. List any public artifacts you know of: publications, talks, open
source, writing.

## 3. The person
Hobbies, interests, obsessions, taste: sports, books, music, films, games,
food, places I've lived or love, languages, causes I care about, recurring
jokes or fascinations. Anything that would make the site feel like a person
rather than a CV.

## 4. Aesthetic taste
Any evidence of my design preferences: sites/apps/products I've admired or
hated, colours, minimal vs maximal, playful vs austere, dark mode or light.
If unknown, say so.

## 5. Site content ideas
Given everything above: what sections, pages, or one-off pieces of content
would suit *me specifically*? (e.g. "a /now page", "a bookshelf", "a page
about X trip", "an interactive Y"). 5–10 ideas, each one sentence, ranked by
how distinctively "Tony" they are.

## 6. Centerpiece concepts
The site will have one signature custom/interactive element. Propose 3–5
concepts that fuse my work and my personality, one short paragraph each, with
a note on what data/content each would need from me. (For context, one idea
already on the table is an interactive gridworld where an agent exploits a
visible reward function and the visitor patches it — feel free to rank
against it, remix it, or beat it.)

## 7. ASK TONY FIRST
The borderline items you excluded, as short neutral one-liners, so I can
choose to opt them in.

Format the whole thing as a single markdown document I can copy out. Title it
`BRIEF.md`.
